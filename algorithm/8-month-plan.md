# 8-Month LeetCode Interview Preparation Plan

This is a customized 8-month preparation plan designed for your career growth and next-opportunity readiness.
It aligns with your background (SWE at Roku, strong data engineering and pipeline experience) and covers a systematic review of your **357 previously solved algorithm problems** along with **71 new high-frequency algorithm problems** (completing the NeetCode 150).

---

## Plan Structure Overview

| Month | Topics Covered | Focus & Goals | Target Count |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Month 1** | **Arrays & Hashing (Basics)** | Re-establish syntax, master lookups, prefix sums, and frequency counts. | ~57 problems |
| **Month 2** | **Two Pointers, Sliding Window, Arrays (Med)** | Master contiguous subarrays, subset matches, and two-pointer pointers. | ~53 problems |
| **Month 3** | **Linked Lists, Stacks & Queues, Bits** | Fast/slow pointers, list design, circular queues, and monotonic stacks. | ~54 problems |
| **Month 4** | **Binary Search, Heaps, System Design** | Binary search on answers, custom sorting, priority queues, and object design. | ~54 problems |
| **Month 5** | **Binary Trees & BSTs** | DFS/BFS traversals, path sums, LCA, BST properties, and height calculations. | ~54 problems |
| **Month 6** | **Graphs (BFS/DFS, Topological Sort), Tries, Backtracking** | Topological sort, course scheduling, islands, prefix trees, and combinations. | ~54 problems |
| **Month 7** | **Greedy Algorithms, Intervals, Arrays (Adv)** | Interval scheduling, greedy choices, and advanced matrix traversals. | ~54 problems |
| **Month 8** | **Dynamic Programming (1D & 2D), SQL Refresh & Mocks** | 1D/2D DP (house robber, coin change, LIS), SQL window functions, and timed mock interviews. | ~48 problems |
